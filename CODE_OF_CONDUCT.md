# Code of Conduct

AI Gateway is built by people with different backgrounds, experience and opinions. We want the project to be a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other place where you represent the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Insults, put-downs, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, [github.com/rockbye-kunal](https://github.com/rockbye-kunal), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are handled privately, and we respect the privacy of whoever reports a problem.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and will explain why when it's appropriate. Depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is, the response can range from a private warning, to a temporary ban from project spaces, to a permanent ban.
